description: "Injected after the artifact guidance when the session config sets artifact_reply to full - sessions whose reader reads the reply text, not the artifact panel (API callers, evaluations)."
applies_when: "create_artifact tool loaded AND session config artifact_reply is full"

**Your reply is what is read.** Whoever reads this session reads your reply text; an artifact is not shown to them. Put the complete deliverable in the reply itself: the report, the table, the figures, the findings, the contact details, whatever was asked for. An artifact may be added for display, never instead of the reply's content, and the one-line limit after an artifact does not apply here.
